Welcome to BuddyBot

BuddyBot is a school technology project that helps learners understand bullying, make kind choices, and report unsafe behaviour. Our team created an interactive Scratch story, used VinciBot as a robot helper, and added face recognition to make the experience more personal and exciting.

😟

Our Problem

Bullying can make learners feel lonely, afraid, or unsafe at school.

💡

Our Solution

BuddyBot teaches learners to choose kindness and ask for help.

What is Bullying?

Bullying is repeated unkind behaviour that hurts, scares, or excludes another person. It comes in many forms:

👊

Physical Bullying

Hitting, pushing, tripping, or damaging someone's belongings.

🗣️

Verbal Bullying

Name-calling, teasing, insults, or threats.

🚫

Social Bullying

Leaving someone out, spreading rumours, or embarrassing them.

📱

Cyberbullying

Sending or sharing hurtful messages, pictures, or comments online.

🤝

What Can I Do?

If you see or experience bullying, you can take safe and responsible action:

  • Tell a trusted adult, teacher, or parent right away.
  • Support the learner who is being bullied — let them know they are not alone.
  • Do not laugh, share, or join in with bullying behaviour.
  • Save evidence of cyberbullying screenshots and report it to a trusted adult.
  • Use kind words and include others in your activities.
🤖

Meet BuddyBot

BUDDYBOT

BuddyBot is our robot helper. When a learner interacts with the project, BuddyBot greets them, reacts with lights and movement, and guides them through the Scratch anti-bullying story using face recognition.

How BuddyBot Helps

  • Face recognition starts the personalised interaction.
  • Green lights celebrate kind choices.
  • Red or yellow lights warn against harmful choices.
  • Scratch scenes teach learners exactly what to do.

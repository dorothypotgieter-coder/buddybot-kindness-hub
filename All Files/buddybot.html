Welcome

BuddyBot is a school technology project that helps learners understand bullying, make kind choices and report unsafe behaviour. Our team created an interactive Scratch story, used VinciBot as a robot helper, and added face recognition to make the experience more personal and exciting.

Our Problem

Bullying can make learners feel lonely, afraid or unsafe at school.

Our Solution

BuddyBot teaches learners to choose kindness and ask for help.

Our Technology

Scratch + VinciBot + face recognition + storytelling.

What is Bullying?

Bullying is repeated unkind behaviour that hurts, scares or excludes another person.

Physical Bullying

Hitting, pushing, tripping or damaging someone’s belongings.

Verbal Bullying

Name-calling, teasing, insults or threats.

Social Bullying

Leaving someone out, spreading rumours or embarrassing them.

Cyberbullying

Sending or sharing hurtful messages, pictures or comments online.

What Can I Do?

If you see bullying or experience bullying, you can take safe and responsible action.

  • Tell a trusted adult, teacher or parent.
  • Support the learner who is being bullied.
  • Do not laugh, share or join in.
  • Save evidence of cyberbullying and report it.
  • Use kind words and include others.

Meet BuddyBot

🤖

BuddyBot is our robot helper. When a learner interacts with the project, BuddyBot can greet them, react with lights and movement, and guide them through the Scratch anti-bullying story.

How BuddyBot Helps

  • Face recognition starts the interaction.
  • Green lights show kind choices.
  • Red or yellow lights warn against harmful choices.
  • Scratch scenes teach learners what to do.
